Market-Mechanics Forward Excursion Audit for GC Replay

You are a market-data analyst working on NinjaTrader Replay (.nrd) files for GC Gold Futures contracts (GC 08-26 / GC 12-26). You have access to more than 20 days of replay data, some of which were used in prior research phases (Market Mechanics V2 and Order Block V3).

Your task is to compute a real forward excursion profile (MFE / MAE / risk-reward) for several mechanically-defined scenarios. Do not optimize, tune, or fit anything to outcome labels after looking at them. Use only definitions already frozen in the existing V2/V3 reports. The goal is not to improve results but to measure whether the robust mechanical behaviors are strong enough to justify building a trading bot.

Requirements
1. Use the frozen definitions from V2/V3: mechanical levels, accepted/failed breaks, first retest, refill latency, Order Block (Classical Final Opposite and Depletion), progressive consumption, flip after confirmed failure.
2. Do not change any threshold, window, or formula once the run starts. Any new parameter needed for MFE/MAE measurement must be declared up front and applied identically across all admitted days.
3. No PnL, win-rate optimization, or outcome-label leakage. No future information may be used at event time.

Scenarios to measure
Compute forward excursion starting at the causal entry tick for:
A. First touch of a defended Classical Final Opposite Order Block.
B. First touch of a Depletion-type Order Block.
C. First retest after an accepted break (15s, 30s, 60s measured once at event time).
D. Fast-refill retest (refill latency <= the frozen 30-second median where the refill × retest penetration interaction applies).
E. Flip after confirmed failure (opposite-side retest of a failed block).
F. Mechanical level accepted-break continuation (V2 accepted break).
G. Baseline control: all first-touch events pooled, to compare against naive/random direction.

Statistics to compute for every scenario
For each scenario, report on the full sample and per split:
- Time windows after event: 10s, 30s, 60s, 2min, 5min.
- MFE in dollars: median, p25, p75, p10, p90.
- MAE in dollars: median, p25, p75, p10, p90.
- Directional excursion share: share of cases where favorable excursion exceeded adverse excursion.
- Reach frequency before stop: percent reaching +$1, +$2, +$4, +$6, +$10 before reaching -$2, -$4, -$6, -$10. Entry cost includes spread (~$0.40 for GC, ~4 ticks) plus $0.10 slippage.
- Profit-close share: percent closing beyond entry + half-spread at each window.
- Cost-adjusted RR: (median MFE - cost) / (median MAE + cost), with cost = $0.50.

Mandatory splits
- Development (approx. 2026-06-01 to 2026-06-17).
- External Validation (approx. 2026-06-18, 22, 23).
- Historical Holdout (approx. 2026-06-25, 26, 29, 30).
- Prospective (new unseen days such as 2026-07-02, 06, 08, 13, 14 if available and not used before).
- Bullish vs Bearish direction.
- Sessions: Asia, Europe, US, Late.
- Volatility regimes: Low, Normal, High.

Data handling
- Exclude days marked MISSING_L2 or PARTIAL_SESSION.
- Do not substitute any date.
- Every result must include sample size n and Insufficient-Data share.
- If a new threshold is needed for measuring MFE/MAE in a window, declare it and apply it uniformly.

Output format
Write one comprehensive English report with clear comparison tables per scenario. At the end include:
1. Numeric summary: which scenario achieves cost-adjusted RR >= 1.0 consistently across splits.
2. Which scenario reaches +$2 before -$4 in >= 55% of cases.
3. Which scenario reaches +$4 before -$4 in >= 50% of cases.
4. Final recommendation: whether the data justifies building a standalone automated bot.
